================================================================================
RATIONALE
================================================================================

Why Replace Google Cloud with Gmail/Google Drive?

1. TARGET APP ALIGNMENT
   - Our research focuses on specific enterprise applications
   - Gmail and Google Drive are actual collaboration/productivity apps
   - "Google Cloud" is infrastructure, not an application

2. CONSISTENCY WITH OTHER TRACKING
   - We track Salesforce (app), not Salesforce Platform
   - We track Slack (app), not Slack infrastructure
   - We track Jira/Confluence (apps), not Atlassian Cloud

3. BETTER GRANULARITY
   - Gmail and Google Drive can be adopted separately
   - Separate tracking provides more insight into Google Workspace adoption
   - More comparable to Microsoft 365/Azure approach

4. TARGET APPS FOCUS
   - Original research goal: Gmail, Google Drive, Slack, Jira, etc.
   - These are the applications users interact with daily
   - Infrastructure choices are captured separately
